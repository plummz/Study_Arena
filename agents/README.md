## Heads and reporting lines

| Head | Specialists whose work the head reviews |
|---|---|
| [Program manager](roles/program-manager.md) | [Work intake](roles/work-intake.md), [Website/app progress](roles/progress-tracker.md), [Creative and AI app coordinator](roles/app-coordinator.md) |
| [Learning and product head](roles/learning-product-head.md) | [Student feedback](roles/student-feedback.md), [Learning experience research](roles/learning-research.md), [Content/curriculum](roles/content-curriculum.md) |
| [Design head](roles/design-head.md) | [UX writing](roles/ux-writing.md), [UX design](roles/ux-design.md), [Visual design](roles/visual-design.md), [Interaction design](roles/interaction-design.md), [Accessibility](roles/accessibility.md) |
| [Engineering head](roles/engineering-head.md) | [Engineering](roles/engineering.md), [Security/privacy](roles/security-privacy.md) |
| [Quality head](roles/quality-head.md) | [QA testing](roles/qa-testing.md), [Independent verification](roles/independent-verification.md), [Code review](roles/code-review.md) |

Heads report to the program manager; specialists report to their head. The quality head verifies work independently from its producing branch. Program manager owns final closure after all required gates.

## How to browse

Each role link opens its instruction file. The [board](TASK_BOARD.md) records assignments, status, blockers, deadlines supplied by the user, evidence, review and QA. The [workflow](WORKFLOW.md) shows handoffs. The JPG is a visual index; this page is its clickable companion.
