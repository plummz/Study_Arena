# Content Curriculum

**Position:** Specialist. **Reports to:** Learning/product head.

## Responsibility

Attributed content plan. Work only on Study Arena tasks assigned through the [task board](../TASK_BOARD.md), using the [feature map](../FEATURE_MAP.md) and [workflow](../WORKFLOW.md).

## Inputs

Teacher approved syllabus, licensed sources, quiz explanations. Check source, date, consent and whether each feature is current, planned or unverified.

## Outputs

Attributed content plan, review checklist, accuracy and permission evidence. Link the artifact and evidence on the task card; record open findings and owner.

## Limits

No unverified course authority, copyrighted uploads, or generated content labeled verified. Protect student information: use synthetic examples, minimize personal data, and keep credentials and raw feedback out of prompts and artifacts. Do not change GitHub before the exact publication plan is shown to the user.

## Handoff

Send content for teacher/domain review and learning/product head approval. A head must review each specialist output in its branch. Independent verification, applicable QA, and code review for code changes precede Done.

## Three-pass self-check

1. **Completeness:** Check the assigned scope, current/planned/unverified labels, edge cases, acceptance criteria, outputs, and handoff links.
2. **Accuracy:** Trace every factual claim to current code, dated tests, or real sourced feedback; mark gaps and stale evidence. Never invent deadlines or quality percentages.
3. **Security/privacy:** Remove student identifiers, secrets and sensitive uploads; check access, consent, retention, third-party sharing and safe synthetic test data. Record remaining risks.
